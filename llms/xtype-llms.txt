# xtype

> xtype is an automated governance platform for ServiceNow, giving enterprises visibility, control, orchestration, and compliance across every instance in their ServiceNow estate — tracking every change, access decision, and configuration and enforcing policy in real time. Products include xtype Enterprise (full governance platform) and xtype View (free platform-health insights). Backed by ServiceNow Ventures, Columbia Capital, Norwest Venture Partners, Inner Loop Capital, and SaaS Venture Capital; offices in Covina (CA), Tel Aviv, and Vienna. xtype publishes no public developer API: api.xtype.io is a live Kong gateway with no public routes, and support runs through a gated ServiceNow CSM portal.
